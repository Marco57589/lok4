# Lokfor (`LOK4`)

> **Local-first, offline-first utility apps for everyday life.**

**Lokfor** (stylized as **LOK4**) is an independent suite of lightweight Android apps built with a simple philosophy: **your data belongs on your device, not in the cloud.**

---

## ✨ Core Principles

- **100% Offline-First:** Everything works without an internet connection.
- **Privacy by Design:** Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero accounts required.
- **No Ads, No Bloat:** Completely free and distraction-free.
- **Local Data:** Full control over your files and backups on your own device.
