
# Lokfor (LOK4)

> **Local-first, offline-first utility apps for everyday life.**

**Lokfor** (stylized as **LOK4**) is an independent suite of lightweight Android applications built around a simple philosophy: **your data belongs on your device, not in the cloud.**

Designed to be simple, private and accessible, Lokfor provides everyday tools without unnecessary complexity, intrusive advertising or mandatory online services.

---

## ✨ Core Principles

- **Offline by Design:** Apps are designed to work without an internet connection.
- **Privacy by Design:** No tracking, no analytics and no unnecessary data collection.
- **No Ads, No Bloat:** Free, lightweight and distraction-free applications.
- **Local Data:** Personal information stays on your device, under your control.
- **Simple & Functional:** Essential features, intuitive interfaces and no unnecessary complexity.
- **User Ownership:** Your files and data remain yours, with local storage and backup options.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

Privacy is not an optional feature. It is the foundation of Lokfor.

- No mandatory accounts or registration.
- No advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No analytics or telemetry.
- No cloud synchronization requirements.
- No collection or transmission of personal data by the apps.
- Local storage and user-controlled backups.

Each application is designed to operate independently, without requiring a centralized Lokfor account or cloud infrastructure.

**Your device. Your data. Your control.**

---

## 🛠️ Development Philosophy

Lokfor focuses on building lightweight and efficient Android applications.

- Native Android development.
- Modern Android UI and platform technologies.
- Minimal dependencies and reduced application footprint.
- No unnecessary background processes.
- Efficient battery and resource usage.
- Open and standard file formats whenever practical.

The goal is to provide useful everyday applications while keeping the software simple, maintainable and respectful of device resources.
